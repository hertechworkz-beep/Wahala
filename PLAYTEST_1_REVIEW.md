# Wahala: Lagos — Playtest 1 Review (Chief Emeka, full run)

Basis: the creator's own playthrough on an Android phone (portrait, in-app browser), 20 screenshots from Day 2 to the Verdict Card, plus her direct feedback. This review describes what was visible; it does not claim to verify code behaviour.

## The creator's verdict, in her words

"I played it through, but I didn't feel anything. I was even tired of reading. It was read, click, read, click. It's like a questionnaire. Where's the rush? Where's the things you're doing? There's no noise, nobody talking, just rain sound and one tiny moving vehicle. I'm not seeing Chief. It's just showing me a picture, then pick something. It's so flat."

## What the player experienced

1. No emotional connection: no excitement, suspense, attachment or satisfaction.
2. Excessive reading: read a passage, pick a response, repeat.
3. Very little agency: dressing up, investigating, travelling and meeting people were described, not performed.
4. Characters didn't feel alive: Chief was mostly a contact label, a transcript or a static cartoon.
5. Weak immersion: repetitive rain ambience; Lagos didn't feel busy or interactive.
6. Same rhythm every day: text, choices, notifications, identical day-end screen.
7. Unclear rewards: Wallet, Sanity and Clout changed without clear reasons.
8. Big events were reported, not shown.
9. City spots (Glow Up, Bestie, Club, Pray) were menus leading to more text.
10. No urge to keep playing; she finished only to complete the test.

## Screen by screen

1. **Day 2, bank alert + voice note.** Alert "Credit ₦42,500. Crypto pumped small." Chief is a circle with the letter C; a 0:20 voice-note player. Large empty rainy background. A voice note should feel like Chief, not an item to process.
2. **Day 2, the cousin and the loan form.** Narration says a girl holds a folder "LOAN APPLICATION — GUARANTOR COPY", but only a male character is shown; no girl, no folder. Salon setting isn't interactive. Three text buttons.
3. **Day 3, family message.** "Phone will be off. Family things." Three text replies. No visual drama of the family situation.
4. **Day 3, the guarantor document.** Narration: "Page 4 says Guarantor. Page 5 says Apapa Port Demurrage Facility." No document shown; Chief's reaction ("He takes the pen back. Slowly.") is written, not acted. Strongest missed chance for an inspect gameplay moment.
5. **Day 3 end.** "Night falls on Lagos. Day 3 survived." City spots menu + Sleep. Activities are instructions, not places.
6. **Day 4, crypto loss + gossip.** Binance "down ₦29,500" and a Parlour gist notification, then another voice-note player. The money loss doesn't feel like anything.
7. **Day 4, Musa transcript.** Voice note shown as text. Musa could be a memorable character; he's a sentence.
8. **Day 4, traffic officer.** The most populated scene (POS, Recharge, Suya, Mama Put stalls, a danfo). Still three text buttons. Option says "₦10k" but shows "-₦13k" with no explanation.
9. **Day 4 end.** Same day-end screen as Day 3.
10. **Day 5, Barrister Okafor.** Text exchange and three replies; the lawyer never appears.
11. **Day 5 end.** Wallet, Sanity and Clout all rose; no explanation why.
12. **Day 6, Chief's transfer.** Alert says ₦18,000; narration says "₦20k lands." Mismatch.
13. **Day 6, the Prado repossession.** Narration: "A tow truck is already hooking the Prado." No tow truck or Prado shown. This should be a major visual set piece.
14. **Day 6, traffic.** The jam isn't really shown; okada option is just a text result.
15. **Day 6 end.** Same day-end screen; Sanity rose 35% to 43% with no reason given.
16. **Day 7, noodles.** Player "sends a picture of noodles"; no picture appears. Alert ₦13,000 vs narration ₦15k.
17. **Day 7, end of Phase 1.** "You're exclusive. For now." The best line in the run, but a static ending with no emotional payoff.
18. **Verdict Card.** "Senior Clown, Island Branch." Funny and shareable, but: "Spent ₦0, got ₦116.5k back, net +₦150k" doesn't reconcile; goal "Secure the Bag" marked failed despite a gain, without explanation; "3 red flags missed" while the player clearly caught the guarantor clue; "Only 70.8% of simulated runs" needs to be a real computed figure.
19. **Share controls.** WhatsApp, IG Story, X, copy link, downloads. Untested here.
20. **Phase 2 teaser + trophy shelf.** WhatsApp field (keep consent clear), 10 locked trophies, "Date Chief again (new truth)". Replay must mean genuinely different events, not reworded text.

## Cross-screen problems

- Lots of empty space; the scene and the chat panel feel like two separate apps.
- The same red-capped character everywhere, few expressions, no movement.
- Dialogue mentions things that aren't on screen (girl with folder, document, tow truck, noodles photo, traffic jam).
- Every situation uses the same three outlined buttons.
- Notifications are doing the work gameplay should do.
- Money numbers don't match across alert, text, meter and Verdict.
- Identical day-end screens.
- Sound is a single repetitive rain loop; no voices.

## Keep

Chief's personality and hidden truths, the 7-day structure, persistent consequences, Wallet/Sanity/Clout (with clearer feedback), Lagos humour, the Parlour and phone, clues, the Verdict Card and Receipts, trophies and replay, in-world ad slots.

## The fix

Move from telling the player what happened to letting them experience it. Follow BRIEF.md's "Playtest 1 verdict and gameplay overhaul", "Adrenaline: action set pieces" and "Voice plan". Rebuild Chief's first date first. Acceptance test: after playing it, does the creator feel she took part in something, or read about it?
