# Lagos Life research: what it does, where it falls short, how Wahala wins

Prepared October 9, 2026 from public reporting. Read alongside BRIEF.md.

## What Lagos Life is

- A free browser life-simulation set in a virtual Lagos, built by Shalom Mathew in under 12 hours and launched October 1, 2026. Players work, earn money, furnish homes, travel around the city and interact with thousands of other players. ([Condia](https://thecondia.com/shalom-mathew-lagos-life))
- Players create a character with a starting position of "Nepo" (wealth and connections) or "Lapo" (microfinance-loan hustle), then work jobs, manage energy, buy food, pay rent and socialise in a shared Lagos with landmarks like Amala Shitta and Quilox. The virtual day follows the real day. ([WeeTracker](https://weetracker.com/2026/10/06/lagos-life-game-nigeria-browser-gaming-explodes/))
- There is no single story; you are given a life to manage and decide what to do with it. ([Condia](https://thecondia.com/lagos-life-game/))
- Scale: over 2 million players, over 145,000 concurrent users and 16.5 million visits by October 5, with Abuja and Port Harcourt added. About USD 46.9K revenue in four days. ([WeeTracker](https://weetracker.com/2026/10/06/lagos-life-game-nigeria-browser-gaming-explodes/))
- Money: roadside billboards at ₦5K a week (38 slots), a lagoon plot at ₦18K, and ₦50K for artists to have music played in virtual clubs. ([WeeTracker](https://weetracker.com/2026/10/06/lagos-life-game-nigeria-browser-gaming-explodes/))

## Why it worked

1. No download: the link is the product, which matters in a market with expensive data and only about 3.5% credit card penetration.
2. Instant familiarity: the world looks like the one outside the window.
3. Revenue from day one through in-world advertising.

## Where it fell short

1. **Outage on day two** (October 2); every player was compensated with ₦100K in virtual cash.
2. **Economy exploit:** players generated vast sums of fake naira; balances had to be reset and income tax added.
3. **Retention is uncertain:** reporting notes that viral browser games often lose players once the novelty fades.
4. **No story or ending:** a sandbox gives nothing to finish and nothing designed to share.
5. **Copycats arrived within days** (a Port Harcourt life sim, a London bus game), so being first with a theme is not a moat.

## How Wahala wins (all written into BRIEF.md as requirements)

| Lagos Life | Wahala |
| --- | --- |
| Open sandbox, no ending | Story with stakes, twists and a Verdict every run |
| Small sprites, map clicks | Expressive characters, cinematic living scenes |
| Hacked economy | Server-validated money, purchases and rewards |
| Day-two outage | CDN-hosted PWA that keeps playing if servers struggle |
| Needs bars and chores | Pure drama; real-life burdens only as story beats |
| Screenshots of houses | Designed Verdict Card and Receipts built to be posted |
| Billboards beside the road | Brands inside the story: songs in the club, venues for dates |
| One city, then imitators | Relationship drama as the moat; cities become Seasons |

Wahala's moat is not "Lagos": anyone can copy a city. It's the relationship drama, the characters people argue about, and the Receipts people post.

## Notes on Lagos Life's screens (from Gemini's description; not independently verified)

Gemini describes Lagos Life as a flat 2D map with clickable venue pins (Amala Shitta, Quilox, Balogun Market, Eko Hotels), Sims-style needs bars, menu-based action timers ("Work shift", "Eat amala"), rent due weekly, and a live venue chat. If accurate, it confirms the gap: Lagos Life is a dashboard you manage; Wahala is a scene you live in.

## Brands

Lagos Life proved Nigerian brands and artists will pay for in-game presence from day one. Wahala's inventory (see BRIEF.md, "Brand inventory") puts brands inside the story itself and lets them book and track placements on a self-serve page.
