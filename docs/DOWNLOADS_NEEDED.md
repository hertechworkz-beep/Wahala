# Two downloads needed for the 3D proof

I'm sourcing everything else myself:
- **Characters:** MakeHuman bodies, built here in Blender (CC0).
- **Animations:** Microsoft Rocketbox (MIT).
- **Furniture, rooms, the bridge and the city:** modelled here in Blender.
- **Night lighting:** Poly Haven HDRIs, via the three.js repository (CC0).

This environment can't reach the two sites below.

## 1. MakeHuman system assets (eyebrows, eyelashes, hair, skins, clothes)

- Open https://static.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/makehuman_system_assets.html
- Click the download link for the zip (it's CC0, free for any use).
- Upload the zip here as it is.

This pack gives Chief realistic eyebrows, eyelashes, age-appropriate skin textures and hair, and gives the player avatars hairstyles and clothes. Without it, I make these myself, and they'll look plainer.

If the upload is refused for size, unzip it and upload only these folders, zipped together: `eyebrows`, `eyelashes`, `hair`, `skins`, `teeth`, `tongue` and `clothes`.

## 2. Musa's car (an SUV in the Toyota Land Cruiser Prado style)

- Go to https://sketchfab.com and sign in (a free account works).
- Search for **Toyota Land Cruiser Prado** (or **Toyota Prado**, or **Land Cruiser**).
- In the filters, tick **Downloadable**.
- Pick a model whose licence says **CC Attribution** or **CC0**; it's shown on the model's page. Don't use "Editorial" or "Standard" licences.
- Prefer a model under about 100,000 triangles; the count is shown on the page. Black or dark grey is ideal, but I can recolour it.
- Click **Download 3D Model**, then choose **glTF** (the .zip with a .gltf or .glb file inside).
- Upload that zip here, and tell me the model's page link so I can credit the artist.

If there's no Prado you like, any black SUV of similar size will do.
